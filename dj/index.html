Autopilot DJ

Listen. Every track is analysed here in your browser: tempo, beat grid, the first beat of each bar, key, and where the intro, breakdowns, drops and outro sit.

Choose. The next track is picked for key, tempo and energy, and each handover is planned from what the two tracks allow — a long blend, a drop swap, or an echo out.

Glide. Both decks share one tempo curve, so beats stay locked while the tempo eases over. Fades are shaped, never straight lines.

Signal processing and rules, not a neural network; nothing is uploaded. It copies the techniques of club DJing, not the taste. Analysis is an estimate — fix a wrong key or downbeat from a track’s Edit button.
