Autopilot DJ

drop in tracks — it plays the set
1 · Listen

Tempo, beat grid, the first beat of every bar, key (Camelot) and where the intro, breakdowns, drops and outro are.

2 · Choose

The next track by key fit, tempo and energy. Then a move: bass swap, drop swap or echo out.

3 · Lock

Both decks follow one tempo curve, so bar lines stay aligned while tempo glides to the new track’s own.

4 · Perform

Every EQ swap, sweep, roll and riser is placed on the audio clock ahead of time, so a busy page can’t make it late.

Signal processing and rules — not a neural network, nothing uploaded. It copies the techniques of club DJing; it has no taste or crowd sense. Analysis is an estimate: fix a wrong key or downbeat on the track row. Tempo changes are pitch-coupled, like vinyl.
