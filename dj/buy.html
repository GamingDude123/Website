What it does

Mixes for you

It listens to each song, picks the order and the transition points, and blends one into the next with crossfades, filter swaps, stutters and mashups.

Sound effects

Risers, sweeps and other effects are dropped in at the transitions on their own.

Turntable decks

Take over any time: scrub, rewind, fast-forward, brake and spin back.

Vocal tools

Cut the vocals out of a track, or keep only the vocals.

Moods

One-click vibe presets change how bold or smooth the mixing is.

Playlists and export

Import a Spotify or SoundCloud playlist and it matches the songs to files you own. Save the finished mix as a WAV file.

Questions

What do I need?

A Mac or a Windows PC, and your own music files. No songs are included. You need an internet connection to activate your license, and the app checks it again now and then.

Does it run on my phone or in a browser?

No. This is a desktop app for Mac and Windows only.

Are my songs uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs on your computer and your files never leave it.

How does the license work?

After you buy, you get a license key by email. Paste it into the app to unlock it. One key works on up to 2 of your computers, and you can move it by deactivating one from the License menu.

Why might my computer show a security warning?

The app is not code-signed yet, so macOS and Windows may warn about an unknown developer the first time you open it. On a Mac, right-click the app and choose Open. On Windows, choose More info, then Run anyway.
