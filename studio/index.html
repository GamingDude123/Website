Loop Lab record anything — it produces itself

Every take runs through the same chain before it lands on a pad, and each step decides for itself whether it applies:

  1. De-noise. Builds a profile of your room from the quietest moments and subtracts it. If the take is one held sound with no gaps, it backs off instead of hollowing it out.
  2. Trim. Finds the transient and cuts the dead air, keeping a few milliseconds of pre-roll so the attack survives.
  3. Listen. Length, brightness, noisiness and pitch confidence decide whether this is a kick, a hat, a snare, a bassline, a vocal, a chord or a texture.
  4. Tune. Pitched sounds get their fundamental found and moved to the nearest note of your key — pitch shifted, not sped up, so the length stays put. Drums are never tuned.
  5. Fit the grid. Anything loop-shaped is time-stretched to the nearest power-of-two number of beats at your tempo. Beyond about a quarter either way it is left alone rather than mangled.
  6. Rebuild. Pick what the sound should be and it is rebuilt as that instrument, not just filtered like one. The instrument's envelope is forced onto your recording — which is what turns a two-second noise into an 80 ms hi-hat — and a synthesised version is layered underneath: a sub whose pitch collapses onto the root for a kick, metallic top end for a hat, drum body and wire noise for a snare, an oscillator following your phrasing for a bass. How much crossfades between your recording and the instrument, so what survives is your attack and your timbre.
  7. Shape. Role-specific EQ, saturation and compression — the low-mid dip and presence lift on a vocal, the 60 Hz lift and 400 Hz cut on a kick.
  8. Arrange. Each pad gets a part that suits what it is, the kick ducks everything under it, and the whole thing goes through glue compression and a limiter.

All of it runs in this tab — the maths is in js/dsp.js and the decisions are in js/polish.js. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, there is no API key, and it works with the aeroplane mode on. Every automatic decision is listed on the pad and can be overridden.
