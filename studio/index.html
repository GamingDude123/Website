How it works

  • Hold an empty pad to record into it. Let go and it is on the pad, cleaned up and ready.
  • Tap a pad to play it. Hold a full pad to open it.
  • MIC at the top lets you record over a pad that already has something.
  • ● next to play arms the loop: everything you tap gets written in, snapped to the grid. Press it again to stop.
  • FILTER and ROLL work while you hold them.
  • Open a pad to rebuild it as a kick, a hat, a bass — whatever you want it to be.

Everything runs in this tab. Nothing is uploaded, there is no account and no key, and it works with the aeroplane mode on.
